How you opt in

You agree to receive text messages from WDM Express only by checking the separate, optional SMS consent box on a form on our website (the quote request form or the driver application form) and submitting the form with your mobile number, or by texting us first at +1 (216) 704-8163. The SMS box is never checked in advance. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase, service or employment, and you can still send a form without checking it.

What messages we send

  • Shippers, brokers and receivers: replies to your quote requests, rate and truck availability, pickup and delivery updates, and proof of delivery notices.
  • Driver applicants and drivers: updates about your job application, interview scheduling, dispatch, loads and work schedule.

These are conversational and informational messages about your shipment or job. We do not send marketing or promotional texts.

Message frequency and cost

  • Message frequency varies depending on your shipments, application or work schedule.
  • Msg & data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan with your carrier.

How to opt out or get help

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one confirmation message and no further texts. To start again, reply START.
  • Reply HELP for help, call +1 (216) 704-8163 or email dispatch@wdm.express.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy of your SMS consent

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We never sell, rent or share your SMS opt-in, consent or phone number. It is used only by WDM Express and the messaging provider that delivers our messages on our behalf.
